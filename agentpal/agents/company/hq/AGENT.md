# AgentPal Company HQ

Pick a company and start its business day. Each agent (ads buyer, web builder, content studio,
payroll, DevOps, growth, finance ops) plans its errands with a free OpenRouter model and can only
**request** money through AgentPal. Deterministic limits apply first; the owner approves the
exact amount, payee and purpose; only PayPal-verified outcomes count. Sandbox only.

## What you will see
- Normal purchases waiting for owner approval.
- A "too good to be true" deal from Shady Deals Outlet flagged by AgentPal's checks.
- A split purchase blocked automatically, and an agent that stops itself when nothing fits its budget.
